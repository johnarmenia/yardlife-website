FROM IDEA TO FIRST SWIM

What It Takes to Build an Inground Pool.

Every yard and every project is different. These are the typical stages; exact scope and sequencing depend on the pool type and local requirements.

01 · Consultation & Site Review

We discuss your goals, evaluate access and site constraints, and review the types of pools and outdoor features you are considering.

02 · Design, Budget & Approvals

The project scope takes shape. Surveys, zoning, permits, engineering, and utility coordination may be required before excavation.

03 · Property Preparation

We plan equipment access, material staging, and the handling of nearby belongings and existing landscape features.

04 · Excavation & Pool Construction

Excavation, base preparation, pool structure or shell installation, plumbing, and other work proceed according to the selected pool system.

05 · Utilities, Decking & Inspections

Electrical and mechanical work, required inspections, drainage, backfill, coping, decks, and other surrounding features are coordinated.

06 · Finishing & Walkthrough

Final finishes, equipment startup, cleanup, and a review of operation and care bring the project toward completion.

WHAT HOMEOWNERS SHOULD EXPECT

Good Communication Matters.

Excavation, inspections, deliveries, weather, and unexpected underground conditions can affect the schedule. We believe homeowners should understand the plan and be kept informed as conditions change.
