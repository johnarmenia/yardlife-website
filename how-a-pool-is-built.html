FROM CONCEPT TO COMPLETION

12 Steps to Your New Backyard Pool.

Every property and pool system is different. The sequence below is a typical overview, not a fixed construction schedule; some activities overlap or change order based on inspections, weather, site conditions and municipal requirements.

STEP 01

Initial Consultation & Site Evaluation

We discuss your goals, pool type, property layout, access, drainage, desired features and overall backyard budget. Site conditions and local zoning requirements help shape a realistic plan.

STEP 02

Design, Renderings & Scope

We develop the pool concept and coordinate the surrounding patio, fencing, landscaping and lighting. Renderings help visualize the design; a defined scope and budget guide decisions.

STEP 03

Survey, Engineering & Permits

A current survey, zoning review and applicable construction plans are assembled. Permits and approvals are obtained as required by the municipality before regulated work begins.

STEP 04

Layout & Site Preparation

The pool location and elevations are established. Access, utility locations, soil handling and material deliveries are coordinated before excavation.

STEP 05

Excavation & Ground Preparation

The pool is excavated to the approved dimensions. Soil conditions, groundwater, drainage and stone base requirements are addressed based on the design and site conditions.

STEP 06

Pool Structure Installation

Vinyl-liner pools typically receive wall panels, bracing, a concrete collar and a finished floor; fiberglass shells are set and leveled on an engineered base; gunite pools use reinforced steel and pneumatically applied concrete. Methods vary by pool system.

STEP 07

Plumbing, Electrical & Bonding

Pool plumbing, drains, skimmers, returns, equipment connections and electrical work are installed. Required bonding, grounding and applicable safety provisions are completed by qualified trades.

STEP 08

Inspections & Testing

Required municipal inspections occur at the appropriate construction stages. Depending on the pool and jurisdiction, these may include structural, collar, plumbing pressure, electrical trench/bonding, gas and final inspections. Work is covered only after required approvals.

STEP 09

Backfill, Coping & Decking

After applicable approvals, the pool perimeter is backfilled and prepared for coping, pavers, architectural concrete or traditional concrete. Drainage and finished elevations are coordinated with the yard.

STEP 10

Fencing, Lighting & Landscaping

Required barriers and gates, low-voltage lighting, planting, irrigation adjustments and other backyard features are installed and coordinated with the overall design.

STEP 11

Interior Finish, Filling & Startup

Vinyl liners, fiberglass finishing requirements or gunite interior finishes are completed as applicable. The pool is filled, circulation equipment is started, and water chemistry is balanced.

STEP 12

Final Inspections & Owner Walkthrough

Final approvals, safety checks, equipment orientation and punch-list items are addressed. The homeowner receives an overview of operation and maintenance before project closeout.

GOOD TO KNOW

What Can Affect the Timeline?

Permit review and inspection availability, weather, access, unexpected soil or groundwater conditions, material lead times and homeowner changes can affect the schedule. We focus on communicating these dependencies and coordinating work as the project progresses.

Construction requirements and inspection sequencing vary by municipality, pool type and approved plans. Your local building department and licensed trades determine applicable requirements.
