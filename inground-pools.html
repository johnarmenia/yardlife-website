YOUR POOL OPTIONS

Three Ways to Make a Splash.

We work across the major inground pool construction types. Each offers different shapes, finishes, timelines, and maintenance considerations.

Fiberglass Pools

Factory-manufactured shells can offer streamlined installation and a smooth, low-porosity surface. Available shapes and sizes depend on the manufacturer.

Vinyl-Liner Pools

Flexible design possibilities with a replaceable interior liner. Liner replacement is a normal long-term ownership consideration.

Concrete / Gunite Pools

Highly customizable shapes, depths, and built-in features. These pools typically involve longer construction and finish-maintenance considerations.

POOL PLANNING FAQ

Questions Worth Asking Early.

The answers will vary by property and municipality. We'll help you identify what needs to be confirmed before work begins.

How long does it take to build an inground pool?

Timelines vary with pool type, design, weather, permit and inspection schedules, site access, and additional hardscape work. A realistic schedule should be discussed after a site review.

Do I need permits?

Inground pools generally require local permits and inspections. Exact requirements—including fencing, electrical, and zoning—depend on your municipality and project.

Will excavation affect my lawn or driveway?

Heavy equipment and soil removal can affect access routes and nearby surfaces. Planning for entry, staging, protection, and restoration is part of responsible construction.

Which pool type is best?

There is no universal answer. Consider your preferred shape, budget, installation constraints, finish options, maintenance, and long-term ownership costs.
