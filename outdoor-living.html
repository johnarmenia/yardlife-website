COMMON QUESTIONS

Planning Your Outdoor Space

Should I plan my pool and patio together?

Yes. Coordinating the layout early helps align drainage, access, coping, and outdoor living areas.

Can existing patios be incorporated?

We review their condition, elevation, drainage, and how they connect to the new design before recommending a plan.

When should lighting and outdoor kitchen utilities be planned?

During design, before hardscape installation, so electrical and utility routes are accounted for.
